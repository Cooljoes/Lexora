Kokoro-82M · English speech synthesis assets

Original weights and English voices: hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, Apache License 2.0.
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M

Core ML conversion, INT8 weights, G2P encoder and decoder, vocabularies,
and the af_heart (US) and bf_emma (GB) voice vectors:
https://huggingface.co/aufklarer/Kokoro-82M-CoreML-INT8/tree/4ef656f55554ceb242ed11febbdbbffd60dd5938
Selected files are unmodified from this revision. The model synthesizes mono
PCM at 24,000 Hz. Core ML inference runs locally on the Apple device.

Misaki English phoneme dictionaries: hexgrad and contributors, Apache License 2.0.
https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki/tree/fba1236595f2d2bf21d414ba6e57d25256afada3/misaki/data
Lexora converted the US and GB gold and silver dictionaries into lexicon.sqlite
for on-demand lookup. Lookup keys are case-folded, gold entries take priority
over silver, and the default pronunciation is stored for entries with variants.
These synthesis tokens are separate from the IPA displayed by the dictionary.

Core ML input and output conventions are documented in the upstream implementation:
https://github.com/soniqo/speech-swift/tree/8effd8f8cbcb6aa1b9355cc6120c2290f05eafd7/Sources/KokoroTTS
